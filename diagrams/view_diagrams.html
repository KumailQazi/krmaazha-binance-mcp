Tier 6
External Exchanges
Binance public Spot & USD-M APIs, news RSS, and macro sentiment feeds.

How a Price Tick Flows (Step-by-Step)

Stage 1
Ingest Raw Data
Pulls Binance Klines (15m, 1h, 4h, 1d), order book bids/asks, and news RSS.
Stage 2
Validate & Clean
Zero-lookahead closed bar filter ([:-1] slicing) + strict Python Decimal typing.
Stage 3
Calculate Signals
Computes Trend (30%), RSI (20%), MACD (20%), %B (15%), and Sentiment (15%).
Stage 4
5-Agent Audit
Data Eng, Security, Protocol, Quant, and UX agents verify capital safety.
Stage 5
Bottom-Line Synthesis
Produces: 'At [timestamp], [asset] is [state]. Setup valid ONLY if [trigger]...'
Stage 6
Output & Execution
Renders on Web Terminal V2 or dispatches to human confirmation gate.
