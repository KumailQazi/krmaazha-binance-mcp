# Token Value Framework — Institutional Value-Capture & Archetype Matrix

The most expensive confusion in crypto: **a successful project does not imply a valuable token.** Adoption headlines pump narratives; only verifiable value *capture* sustains long-term purchasing power. Apply this institutional checklist (`kb://frameworks/token-value`) before holding any crypto asset.

---

## 1. Epistemic Rigor & Claim Classification
Every assertion within an asset thesis MUST carry an epistemic label:
- **`[VERIFIED FACT]`**: Backed by auditable on-chain ledger records, exchange order-book depth, or official regulatory/code filings.
- **`[QUANTITATIVE ESTIMATE]`**: Derived from mathematical formulas (e.g. ATR, Mayer Multiple, Net R:R, staking yields net of dilution) with explicit parameters.
- **`[ANALYST INTERPRETATION]`**: Narrative, forward-looking thesis, competitive comparison, or regulatory risk assessment.

---

## 2. Archetype-Specific Evaluation Matrix
Do NOT force corporate Price/Sales multiples or irrelevant metrics across all tokens. Evaluate each asset under its native economic archetype:

### Archetype A: Monetary Settlement Commodity (e.g., BTC)
- **Primary Metrics**: Hard supply cap (21M), stock-to-flow, network hashrate security, institutional custody/spot ETF net flows, and liquid order-book depth.
- **Value Driver**: Absolute mathematical scarcity, non-sovereign monetary neutrality, and final settlement guarantees.
- **Failure Mode**: Loss of decentralized consensus or catastrophic SHA-256 compromise.

### Archetype B: Execution Layer L1/L2 Utility (e.g., SOL, ETH)
- **Primary Metrics**: Base transaction fee burn vs gross issuance, real economic DEX volume vs wash trading, developer retention, and staking yield **net of annual inflation**.
- **Value Driver**: Direct monetary velocity and fee accrual from compute execution and state bloat pricing.
- **Failure Mode**: Inflation dilution outstripping transaction burns; systemic network outages.

### Archetype C: DeFi Yield & Basis Collateral (e.g., RESOLV, ENA)
- **Primary Metrics**: Total Value Locked (TVL), basis arbitrage spreads, delta-neutral counterparty exposure, oracle dependencies, and protocol net revenue share.
- **Value Driver**: Risk-adjusted yield generation and verifiable collateral stability.
- **Failure Mode**: De-peg of collateral assets, liquidation cascades, or smart-contract exploit.

### Archetype D: Privacy & Alternative Proof-of-Work (e.g., XVG, LTC)
- **Primary Metrics**: Circulating supply ratio, ASIC/GPU mining profitability, merchant settlement velocity, and centralized exchange listing status.
- **Value Driver**: Permissionless micro-payments, optional transaction obfuscation, and unencumbered peer-to-peer transfers.
- **Failure Mode**: Centralized exchange regulatory delisting or 51% hashrate attack.

## 3. The Five Decisive Value-Capture Questions
1. **What does holding the token actually entitle you to?**
   Fee share? Staking yield from real protocol revenue? Governance over a productive treasury? Usage-tied burn? Or only secondary market resale?
2. **Protocol revenue vs fully-diluted valuation (FDV).**
   Compare annualized protocol revenue (not vanity volume or TVL) with FDV. If an asset trades at 100x FDV/Revenue with high inflation, market prices decades of flawless growth.
3. **Supply schedule & unlock cliffs.**
   Examine circulating percentage, foundation vestings, and unlock schedules. Continuous token emissions act as structural sell pressure that no narrative outruns.
4. **Is adoption monetized today?**
   Pilots, testnets, and unpaid partnerships are marketing. Count only integrations that generate measurable fee revenue today.
5. **Competitive moat & fee compression.**
   Can competing forks or Layer-2s offer the same utility for 90% lower fees? Verifiable liquidity depth and moat durability determine survival.

---

## 4. Benchmark Comparison vs. Bitcoin
Bitcoin is the zero-execution-risk baseline. It requires no cash-flow valuation because the asset *is* the monetary product. Any altcoin allocation must offer substantial risk-adjusted outperformance over BTC to justify its stack of smart-contract, governance, and regulatory risks.
